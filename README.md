# Tung Tung Sahur Bat

**doom** × Brainrot · arme · idée de team

Give the player the giant wooden bat of Tung Tung Tung Sahur as a new melee weapon. Every hit launches monsters across the room with a loud tung tung sound. Kills burst into wood chips.

> I gave Doom the Tung Tung Tung Sahur bat: every swing sends a TUNG shockwave that launches demons across the room into wood chips. The Barons fly too.

## Installer

Put mod.pk3 next to GZDoom and start it with: gzdoom.exe -iwad freedoom2.wad -file mod.pk3. You start with the bat selected; press 1 to pick it, fire to swing and launch monsters.
